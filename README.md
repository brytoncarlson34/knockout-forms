# Knockout Forms

A free phone app for Knockout Plumbing. Pick a form, fill it in, the customer signs with a finger, and a branded PDF gets emailed to the customer and the shop. Every form is logged in a Google Sheet shared with the shop.

- Open it at https://brytoncarlson34.github.io/knockout-forms/ and add it to your home screen.
- It only connects to a shop after you open that shop's private setup link, which we text to the shop.
- Open the link in Safari, tap Copy, then Share > Add to Home Screen. Open the app from your home screen and tap Tap to connect.
- We run the back end. Each shop's PDFs and log live in their own Google Drive folder and Sheet, shared with the shop owner.
- No keys or shop info live in this repo.

Free. Made by Bryton.
