# Knockout Forms

A free phone app for Knockout Plumbing. Pick a form, fill it in, the customer signs with a finger, and a branded PDF gets emailed to the customer and the shop. Every form is logged in the shop's own Google Sheet.

- Open it at https://brytoncarlson34.github.io/knockout-forms/ and add it to your home screen.
- It only connects to a shop after you open that shop's private setup link (it comes from the Forms App menu in the shop's Sheet).
- No keys or shop info live in this repo. Customer info stays on the phone and in the shop's own Google account.

Free. Made by Bryton.
